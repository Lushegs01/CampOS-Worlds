# Weapon assets and grip validation

Every weapon in the catalogue (`shared/arena/weapons.mjs`, enumerated at run time, currently 14) has its own
first-person calibration in `src/arena/scene/weaponSpecs.ts`: firing-hand grip, support-hand position, sight line,
magazine well, ejection port, charging point, eye relief and hold style. There is **no fallback**: before this pass a
weapon missing from the table silently borrowed the Vanguard's grip. Now it is not drawn and an error is logged, and a
test keeps the table complete.

## Validator

`tests/arena-weapon-anchors.test.mjs`, backed by `scripts/qa/weapon-assets.mjs` (which reads the real GLB files):

* every catalogue weapon has a calibration, and nothing is calibrated that is not in the catalogue;
* the hold matches the weapon's kind (long gun → `rifle`, pistol → `pistol`, melee → `blade`);
* on long guns the support hand is ahead of the firing hand by at least 15 cm, and the sight is above the bore;
* every anchor lies on the model itself (within 3 cm of its measured bounds);
* both the first-person model and the third-person LOD exist, and the LOD is lighter.

On its first run the validator found a real defect: **the Bastion 12G's sight line was 6.2 cm above the bore, 3 cm
above the top of the gun** (the bead and receiver top are at +3.1 cm, measured on the sheet from
`node scripts/qa/arena-weapons.mjs sheet bastion`). Aiming looked over the gun instead of down its rib. Corrected to
3.4 cm (the top of the bead).

## Moving parts added this pass

`scripts/assets/split-weapon-part.mjs` gained two parts, measured on the 5 cm sheets:

* **Bastion 12G pump forend** (1,788 triangles split out): the animator already slides `pump` back and forward after each
  shell with the support hand riding on it; before this the forend did not move. Frames:
  `docs/screenshots/arena/visual/bastion-pump-cycle.png` (rest, back, returning).
* **Thorn .44 cylinder** (1,792 triangles): turns one chamber per shot (`docs/screenshots/arena/visual/thorn-cylinder.png`).

Triangle counts and texture formats are unchanged; the files grew by 5 KB each.

## Melee: both hands in view

Knife and Breach Axe (`hold: 'blade'`): the weapon is in the firing hand; the support hand is no longer parked below the
frame (its old target was 30 cm under the bore). It holds a **guard**: a closed fist at the lower left of the view
(camera-space target `GUARD` in `src/arena/scene/viewmodel.ts`, hand frame in camera space in `weapons.ts`), which:

* bobs with the stride and pumps when sprinting;
* draws back toward the chest as the blade swings through, and returns on recovery;
* drops with the weapon while switching.

Both fists now close the thumb over the fingers (`thumb` in a hand frame) instead of pointing it up. The knife is
carried turned across the body with the tip raised, so the blade reads along its length.

The Breach Axe model is a 40 cm hatchet, so it is held in one hand with the other in guard. A two-handed haft grip would
not fit a handle that short.

Stills (QA renderer `node scripts/qa/arena-weapons.mjs fp knife,hammer hip,sprint,melee`, which runs the same animator
and arm solver as the game): `docs/screenshots/arena/melee/before-*.jpg` and `after-*.jpg`.

## Not validated by automation

Per-pose clipping (hand through receiver) across every state is checked by eye on the QA renders, not measured. No weapon
inspection animation exists in the game.
